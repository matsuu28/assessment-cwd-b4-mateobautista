THE FACTS BEHIND THE EPISODE (for replies to comments)
- About 10 quintillion (10,000,000,000,000,000,000) insects are alive at any time (Smithsonian estimate).
- About 3 in 4 of the world's leading food crop types benefit from animal pollinators, mostly insects (FAO).
- Wheat, rice and corn are wind-pollinated, so staple grains survive: that's why the world ends up on a grain diet.
- Most songbirds feed insects to their chicks.
- Hand pollination with brushes is already done in some pear and apple orchards (e.g. Sichuan, China).
- The population numbers are a scenario, not a forecast: normal growth for ~3 years on stored food and staples,
  then malnutrition slowly pushes it down to about 7.1 billion by Year 50.
