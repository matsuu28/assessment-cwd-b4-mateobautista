# Earth B: Facebook Page Kit (option 2)

## Name & handle

| Field | Use |
|---|---|
| **Page name** | Earth B |
| **Username** | @earthb.whatif (if taken: @earthb.stories, @theearthb) |
| **Category** | Digital creator (secondary: Video creator, Education) |
| **Action button** | Follow |

**The idea:** Earth B is "Plan B" for the planet. Every episode is the same Earth with one thing changed, then we watch what happens. It's short and bold, works as a logo, and the "B" gives you a ready-made visual hook.

## Bio / Intro (≤101 characters)

> Same planet. One thing changed. 🌍 Weekly 3D What If stories, with real numbers.

Shorter options:
- One change. A whole new world. 🌍 3D What If stories every week.
- Welcome to Earth B: the world, if one thing went differently. 🌍

## About (longer description)

> Earth B is our planet with one rule rewritten. Nobody can die. The Moon disappears. Every phone goes dark at once.
>
> Each 3D animated short follows the fallout from Day 0 onward, with a live world-population counter and real-world numbers at every step.
>
> New episode every week. Tell us what Earth B should change next. 👇

## Look & feel

| | |
|---|---|
| Deep indigo | `#160B2E` (backgrounds, text inside the B badge) |
| Signal coral | `#FF6A4D` (the B badge, seam, buttons, accents) |
| Peach | `#FFB199` (small labels) |
| Alt-Earth purple | `#5A2A8A` · alt land `#FF7A4D` |
| Wordmark | Inter ExtraBold, tight letter-spacing, with "B" in a coral rounded badge |
| Taglines | Libre Baskerville *italic* |
| Signature | Every video opens on the **WORLD POPULATION** counter at `DAY 0` |

How it differs from option 1 (Hypothetica): Hypothetica is calm and editorial (serif type, gold, space at night). Earth B is bolder and more pop-culture (heavy sans-serif type, coral, sunset), which tends to stand out more in a busy feed.
